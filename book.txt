Peter Quill, also known as Star-Lord, adjusted his helmet as he approached the ancient temple. The air on Xandar was crisp and clean, unlike the recycled ship air he was used to.

"Remember the plan," Gamora said, her green skin shimmering in the alien sunlight. "We get in, grab the orb, and get out. No complications."

Rocket Raccoon scoffed from behind a large crystal formation. "No complications? With this crew? You're funnier than I thought, green lady."

"I am Groot," Groot rumbled, his wooden form shifting as he peered around the corner.

"Yeah, I know we need the money," Rocket replied. "But that doesn't mean I have to like taking orders from Mr. Star-Prince over here."

Peter turned to face them, his trademark smirk visible even through his helmet. "First of all, it's Star-Lord. Second, if you have a better plan, furball, I'm all ears."

Gamora stepped between them, her voice tense. "Both of you, stop. The Nova Corps patrol will be here in ten minutes. We don't have time for this."

Drax the Destroyer emerged from the shadows, his massive frame blocking what little light reached the canyon. "I do not understand why we must sneak like cowards. I wish to face our enemies in glorious combat!"

"See? This is what I'm talking about," Rocket said, throwing his small paws in the air. "We've got a walking tree who only says three words, a destroyer who wants to fight everything, a green assassin who's too serious, and a human who thinks he's the boss of me."

Peter activated his jet boots, rising slightly above the group. "You know what? I'm done arguing. Gamora and I will go in first. Rocket, you and Groot create a diversion if things go wrong. Drax... just try not to break anything until we need you to."

"I make no promises," Drax said, a fierce grin spreading across his face.

As Peter and Gamora approached the temple entrance, Peter glanced at her. "You know, for someone who's supposed to be the most dangerous woman in the galaxy, you're not so bad to work with."

Gamora kept her eyes forward. "Don't get sentimental, Quill. This is a business arrangement."

"Hey, I'm just saying—" Peter began, but was cut off as alarms blared throughout the canyon.

Rocket's voice crackled over their comms. "Well, looks like Groot sat on something he shouldn't have. Plan B everyone!"

"I am Groot!" came the distant, slightly embarrassed reply.

Gamora drew her sword. "I told you this would happen."

Peter readied his element guns. "Yeah, but admit it—life's never boring with us, is it?"

Drax let out a joyful roar as he charged toward the first group of Nova Corps officers. "Finally! A worthy challenge!"

As blaster fire filled the air, Rocket scrambled up Groot's shoulders. "Okay, big guy, time to make like a tree and leave! But first, smash anything that's shooting at us!"

Groot extended his branches, creating a protective shield around the smaller raccoon. "I am Groot," he said determinedly.

Peter and Gamora fought back to back, their movements perfectly synchronized despite their constant bickering. "You know," Peter said between shots, "for not wanting complications, you're pretty good at handling them."

Gamora deflected a blast with her sword. "Just focus on not getting us killed, Star-Lord."

"Hey, you said my name right!" Peter said with a grin.

Meanwhile, Rocket was setting up an improvised explosive device. "Groot, when I say now, I need you to throw me toward that big crystal spire. Then get ready to catch me!"

"I am Groot?" Groot asked, sounding concerned.

"Don't worry, I've done this a hundred times! Well, twice. Okay, once. But it worked great!"

Drax was having the time of his life, laughing as he disarmed Nova Corps officers with precise, non-lethal strikes. "Your weapons are useless against me! I am Drax the Destroyer!"

As the battle reached its peak, Rocket executed his plan. "NOW, GROOT!"

Groot launched Rocket through the air like a furry projectile. The raccoon attached his device to the crystal spire and detonated it, creating a massive energy pulse that disabled all the weapons in the area.

The sudden silence was broken by Peter's voice. "Okay, that was actually pretty impressive."

Rocket landed safely in Groot's waiting branches. "See? I told you I had it under control."

Gamora sheathed her sword. "The orb is secured. Let's go before they bring heavier weapons."

As the team regrouped and made their escape, Peter looked at the unlikely group of misfits around him. "You know, we might not be the most professional crew in the galaxy..."

"But we get the job done," Gamora finished, the ghost of a smile on her lips.

"I am Groot," Groot added, nodding sagely.

Rocket climbed onto Peter's shoulder. "Yeah, yeah, we're great. Now can we get paid already?"
